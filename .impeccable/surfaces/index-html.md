## Direction contract

THESIS: The clinic is a place you find your way to, at any age. The site is a Portuguese wayfinding system, signs that point each person to their need, refusing the category's stock-photo hero over a row of service cards.

OWN-WORLD (palette pinned by user from the FisioStudio logo: orange #EF8120, charcoal #424242, white): charcoal sign plates with inset white rules carry direction; logo orange carries the brand and every booking action (booking plate, Marcar buttons, pélvica band, km-post caps); deep orange #A9500A for location and orange text on white; orange tint #FCE3CC ground for Onde estamos; white header with the logo mark. Bold arrow glyphs, simple stroke pictograms, km-post numerals. One sans family (Atkinson Hyperlegible Next), light 300 only for the wordmark echo.
